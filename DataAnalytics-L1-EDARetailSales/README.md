# Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Retail Sales Data

## 📌 Project Overview
The objective of this task is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to uncover actionable business insights, identify underlying sales patterns, and evaluate customer purchasing demographics.

## 🛠️ Technologies & Tools Used
* **Python**
* **Pandas & NumPy** (Data Cleaning & Manipulation)
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (Data Visualization)
* **Jupyter Notebook / VS Code**

## 🔍 Key Steps Performed
1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, corrected data types, and parsed transaction dates.
2. **Descriptive Statistics:** Summary metrics including total revenue, mean transaction value, and category distributions.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:**
   * Monthly and quarterly sales trend analysis.
   * Product category performance evaluation.
   * Customer demographic breakdown (Age & Gender analysis).

## 💡 Key Insights
* **Peak Sales Period:** Sales peaked significantly during end-of-quarter months.
* **Top Product Category:** Electronics generated the highest overall sales revenue.
* **Demographics:** The 25–40 age demographic represents the largest buyer segment.
